# Trademark Guidelines

The software license does not grant rights to the Blam Labs, BlamCore, or Mission Shield names, logos, product marks, or other brand assets except for reasonable attribution and truthful description of the software's origin.

- Official, unmodified releases may be identified as **BlamCore Mission Shield Community Edition**.
- A modified distribution must state that it is modified and must not imply that Blam Labs created, certified, sponsors, or supports the changes.
- A redistributed fork should use a distinct product name and visual identity. It may state that it is based on BlamCore Mission Shield when accurate.
- Branding must not be used in a domain, organization name, package name, or commercial offering in a way that suggests endorsement.

These guidelines are a practical project boundary, not legal advice, and require review before public general availability.
